# Agent Note: Security-research locks a classified product face, not the company

Status: implemented

English | [中文](2026-09-05-security-research-product-face-lock.zh.md)

## Problem

`PLANNER_POLICY` treated Goal-named faces as one locked cluster and held sibling brands as discovered pending until that cluster deepened. Coordinators then serialized an entire company: after recording several independent scope assets they still waited on one explorer page. Same-side search, open-bridge preference, and login-wall limitations lacked a cluster subject, so they read as a company-wide pipeline. Equal-weight accident patches joined into one `join(' ')` policy, so the shortest operational slogan outranked open-world discovery. The aim remains real medium-or-high-impact harm; unknown, long-chain, and compositional paths find issues a checklist would miss and must not outrank a live discriminator.

## Decision

`PLANNER_POLICY` still injects through `security-research:policy` on every Planner request. A locked face is one classified product face, not the whole company. Goal-named hosts that share identity, trust, or implementation form one face and one mapping page. Goal-named hosts already recorded as different assets are different locked faces; each may open its first explorer mapping page without waiting for another face to reach inDepth. Same-side search is a second explorer mapping page on that cluster, not the first mapping page of another registered product face. CDN, generic cloud, and still-unclassified brands found during mapping stay discovered pending; an already-registered independent scope asset is not a sibling brand.

Completable login, including a Goal-supplied OTP or phone number, is a deepening method. A limitation closes a stuck form page after that form has already failed, not the authentication research line. An unauthenticated business API complements a failed wall; it does not replace post-login research.

Depth and breadth stack on different locked faces. Unfamiliar, long-chain, and compositional mechanisms do not outrank a live path that already has a useful discriminator. Preferring to finish one brand before touching another registered product face is not a grounding dependency. Open-bridge growth prefers a chain-bridging dispatch over a new same-side search; another registered, still-untouched product face may still open its first mapping page. Host `CoordinatorAction.next` remains `independent_dispatch_if_different_primary_else_wait`.

The same constant keeps three layers in order: research thesis, locked-face dispatch, then Host control-plane rules. Control-plane text states that it constrains tools and lifecycle and does not prescribe discovery order. Later patches follow that split: lifecycle accidents edit only the control-plane sentences; cadence misreads edit only the locked-face sentences and keep a cluster subject; rewrite the old sentence instead of appending an equal-weight negation. The maintenance split lives in this note, not inside the policy body.

Packet truncation and one-cluster search units remain in [the packet-cluster cadence note](2026-09-04-security-research-packet-cluster-cadence.md). Running-line overlap versus wait remains in [the independent-overlap note](2026-09-04-security-research-independent-overlap-next.md).

## Alternatives considered

**Set `maxIndependentChildren` to 1 or force N explorers.** Rejected because overlap capacity is not a search quota, and a forced N would bomb mapping pages.

**Host-reject a second explorer while any Child is active.** Rejected because that serializes independent product faces; admission stays the last-slot starvation guard.

**Add a coverage service, seed queue, or per-domain slot roster.** Rejected because the Coordinator Session Log already owns durable batches.

**Split thesis and Host ops into extra systemPrompt sections or a policy compiler.** Rejected because that is a second injection path; one `PLANNER_POLICY` constant is enough.

**Rank unknown, long-chain, and compositional work above deepening a live path.** Rejected because the Goal is real medium-or-high-impact harm, not novelty for its own sake.

**Treat every login wall as a limitation that ends authentication research.** Rejected because completable login, including a supplied OTP, is how post-login object and authorization bugs are reached.

## Consequences

Large multi-target Goals may keep one first mapping page per classified product face, typically two or three faces at once, without a second store or scheduler. One cluster still forbids a second mapping page while probed live faces lack inDepth or a Host-bound limitation. Completable login stays in scope. Lifecycle accidents can patch control-plane sentences without rewriting discovery order.
